Biography

I'm a software and firmware developer based in Sover, Italy, with extensive experience designing and implementing complete solutions. My work spans both sides of 3D printing: software — including user interfaces and algorithms — and hardware, from printer assembly and calibration to technical support.

I also have solid electronics skills, covering circuit troubleshooting and repair as well as PCB development. I enjoy projects that connect code to the physical world, and I work mainly with Java, Python, and C/C++, embedded STM32 and Arduino platforms, 4D Systems and DWIN interfaces, and web technologies such as HTML, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL.

For hardware and product design I use Altium for PCB layout, AutoCAD and Inventor for 2D/3D technical drawing, and CorelDraw for graphics. I know the main 3D printing workflows well — Simplify3D, Cura, Orca Slicer, and Bambu Lab — and I'm currently developing HikingLife, an Android GPS navigator for hiking.
